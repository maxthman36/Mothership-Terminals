SYCORAX

NEON PINK PILL · 750cr A DOSE · GROWN NOWHERE ELSE

A powerful drug made only from fruit that grows in one place in all the Dream. Three fruits yield a batch of five hundred pure doses. Strong sense of euphoria for cybermod users.

  • Installation: +20 to Body Saves made when installing cybermods.
  • Mending: Heals Xd10 damage, where X is the number of slots of cybermods and Slickware you have installed.
  • The Count: Every time you take Sycorax, make a mark and roll 1d10. If you roll equal to or lower than the number of marks you have made, you gain a random Mutation, and the count resets to zero.
  • Addictive. The Sun does not invoice the field. Sycorax is not the Sun.

Sold at this market by the Church's own stalls. If anyone offers it cheaper elsewhere on the station, ask them where their fruit grows.
