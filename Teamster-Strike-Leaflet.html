Prospero's Dream · Dockside · Engineering · Haulage
"Everything on this station got here on a Teamster's back."
They took our ships.
They are killing our people.
The boss won't pay.

SHARPE CO. HOLDS A FLEET OF LOCAL FREIGHTERS AND EVERY SOUL ABOARD THEM. THIS WEEK THEY SENT US THE FOOTAGE TO PROVE WHAT THEY DO TO HOSTAGES. YANDEE HAS SEEN IT TOO. YANDEE'S ANSWER WAS NO.

Our members are being held for ransom by Sharpe Co. Not cargo. Not hulls. People. Drivers, loaders, engineers, with families in Dockside and Doptown who are waiting on a knock at the door.

Sharpe has begun sending recordings of executions. They want the whole station to understand the price of waiting. We understand it fine. We are done waiting.

This Local moves the freight, runs the engineering decks that keep the Dream breathing, and hauls for every outfit on this station, including the Golyanovo. There is no cargo on Prospero's Dream that a Teamster did not touch. That is not a boast. It is a reminder of what stops when we stop.

Yandee refuses to pay the ransXXXX rescue money. Yandee refuses to send Tempest Company. The Local cannot raise a rescue fleet out of dues, and everyone at that table knows it. So the crews on those ships are to be written off, because the arithmetic is easier that way.

We do arithmetic too. Here is ours: a union that lets its people be murdered on camera, one at a time, while the boss counts the savings, is not a union anymore. It is a mailing list.

Reidmar has carried our demand to Yandee in person. It is the same demand printed below. There will not be a second, softer version of it.

++ Our Demands ++

  1. Pay the ransom, or send Tempest Company. One or the other, and now. Bring our fleet and our people home.
  2. No more hostage arithmetic. No member of this Local is a line item to be written off in a back room.
  3. Until our people are home, nothing is normal. Members will be kept informed by hall notice and handbill only. Trust what you are handed. Do not trust what you are told.
Strike Authorization Vote
ALL MEMBERS IN GOOD STANDING. UNION HALL, DOCKSIDE.
BRING YOUR O2 CREDSTICK. BRING A COWORKER.
IF YANDEE WILL NOT MOVE, WE WILL. ALL OF US, OR NONE OF US.
32819L
REIDMAR
